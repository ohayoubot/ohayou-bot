    One ration a day, and the land you buy with it

    A bot sits in a chatroom and hands one ration to anyone who says good morning to it. Rations buy acres; acres carry cats, quarries, workshops and refineries. This office surveys what everyone builds and files it here, to scale, one tile to the acre.

    Joining the register

    The bot keeps office in a channel on IRC. IRC is a chat network older than the web and there is nothing to sign up for: you pick a name and connect.
