      Getting in

      1. 01

        Open a chat window

        IRC is a chat network older than the web. There is nothing to sign up for: pick a name, connect, done. The bot keeps office in a channel on IRC.

        or in a new tab

        Nothing loads from the chat network until you press it.

      2. 02

        Say !ohayou

        Twelve ohayous, which is exactly the price of your first cat, and a parcel on the map with your name over it. Every day after that is 0 to 6 more, plus whatever your items have earned you.

        The handbook is the rest of it: every item, cheapest first, and what the crafted ones need.

      3. 03

        Say !web

        The bot sends you a link, privately, good once. It signs you in here for a month so you can read your own file and fly a deer over your land.

        You have to be registered with the bot first: !register, then !identify.
