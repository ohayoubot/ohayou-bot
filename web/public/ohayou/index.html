    Key

      A parcel is drawn the size of the holding it describes and carries its holder's nick unless they asked otherwise. One drawn as Anonymous is bare ground: the acreage, and nothing that says whose it is. Ohayous on hand, vaults and defences are never drawn, named or not.

      Filing a claim

      Land is filed against a services account, not a nick, so nobody takes yours by taking your name. Your parcel carries your nick by default. Say !territory off to the bot and it is drawn as Anonymous instead, acreage still shown, nothing that says it is yours. !territory on puts the name back.

      New to it? The handbook says how a ration works and what there is to spend it on, every item drawn.

      Already playing? Say !web to the bot. It sends you a link that signs you in here for a month.
