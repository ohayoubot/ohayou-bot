The ohayou land registry

Every acre anyone has claimed, surveyed to scale. One tile is one acre. Land is bought with rations, and rations are handed out in a chatroom, one per person per day.

Key

    A parcel is drawn at the size of the holding it describes. Unfiled parcels are shown as bare ground: the registry knows the acreage, not the owner. Ohayous on hand, vaults and defences are never published.

    Filing a claim

    Parcels are filed under a services account, not a nick, so nobody can take yours by taking your name. Say !territory on to the bot and the registry prints your nick on your land; !territory off takes it back off.

    Already playing? Say !web to the bot and it sends you a one-shot link that signs you in here. You will need to be registered and identified with it first.
