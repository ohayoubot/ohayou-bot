Claiming a plot

Every player is on this map. A plot only carries a name when its owner says it can: until then it shows how much land they hold and roughly what they have earned, and nothing about who they are.

To put your name on yours, say this to the bot:

!territory on

!territory off takes the name back off. Your ohayous on hand, your vault and your defences are never shown to anyone but you.
